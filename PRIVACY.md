# 3Cal 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-07-04**　**最近更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-22**

## 简体中文

3Cal 是一款 macOS 菜单栏日历与提醒事项应用。我们非常重视你的隐私，原则很简单：**3Cal 不收集任何数据。**

- **不收集、不上传任何个人信息。** 3Cal 没有账号系统，不含任何分析、统计或广告 SDK。开发者没有服务器，收不到你的任何数据；应用只在下面「联网」列出的几种情形下访问网络。
- **日历与提醒事项数据**：3Cal 通过 Apple 系统框架 EventKit 在你的设备本地读写日历和提醒事项。这些数据始终保存在你的设备及你的 iCloud 账户中（由 Apple 的 iCloud 同步机制管理），开发者无法访问。
- **联网**：3Cal 只在以下情形访问网络，请求直接发往对应的服务方，不经过开发者：
  - **iCloud 同步**：成就、关注、计时与「用 Dot 叫醒」的选择，通过你自己的 iCloud 账户（键值存储）在你的设备之间同步，由 Apple 管理，开发者无法访问。
  - **天气**：外观选「天空」并选择了城市时，3Cal 会把这座城市的坐标发给 Apple 的天气服务（WeatherKit）以取得当地天气，用来决定天空的颜色。城市由你手动选择，3Cal 不请求定位权限、不读取你的实际位置。天气数据由  Weather 提供。
  - **地点搜索**：给日程或提醒事项添加地点、或选择天空的城市时，你输入的关键词会发给 Apple 地图以返回候选地点。
  - **链接**：备注或标题里带网址时，3Cal 会向该网址所在的网站请求站点图标与网页标题，用于显示。
- **权限**：3Cal 仅在你明确授权后访问日历与提醒事项；你可随时在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」中撤销授权。
- **本地偏好**：应用设置（如显示选项、快捷键、所选城市）仅保存在你设备的本地偏好文件中。

如本政策有更新，我们会在此页面发布新版本。

## English

3Cal is a macOS menu-bar app for calendars and reminders. Our privacy principle is simple: **3Cal collects no data.**

- **No personal information is collected or transmitted.** 3Cal has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data; the app accesses the network only in the cases listed under "Network" below.
- **Calendar & reminder data**: 3Cal reads and writes your calendars and reminders locally on your device through Apple's EventKit framework. This data stays on your device and in your iCloud account (synced by Apple's iCloud), and is never accessible to the developer.
- **Network**: 3Cal accesses the network only in the following cases. Requests go directly to the respective provider, never through the developer:
  - **iCloud sync**: Achievements, follows, timers and "Wake with Dot" picks sync between your devices through your own iCloud account (key-value storage), managed by Apple and inaccessible to the developer.
  - **Weather**: When the appearance is set to Sky and you have chosen a city, 3Cal sends that city's coordinates to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) to get local weather, which is used to color the sky. The city is one you pick by hand; 3Cal never asks for location permission or reads your actual location. Weather data is provided by  Weather.
  - **Place search**: When you add a place to an event or reminder, or choose the city for the sky, the keywords you type are sent to Apple Maps to return matching places.
  - **Links**: When a note or title contains a web address, 3Cal requests the site icon and page title from that website for display.
- **Permissions**: 3Cal accesses Calendars and Reminders only after you explicitly grant permission, which you can revoke at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- **Local preferences**: App settings (display options, shortcuts, the chosen city) are stored only in local preference files on your device.

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page.
